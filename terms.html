About our service

Drive With T.K. connects CDL-A drivers with carrier partners. Job details we share (such as pay and home time) describe specific routes as told to us by carriers and can change. Applying does not guarantee an interview, a job or any pay; every carrier makes its own hiring decision.

SMS program: Drive With T.K. Job Alerts

  • How to join: text JOBS to (318) 748-2553, or agree on a phone call with us to receive texts. Joining is optional and not a condition of applying.
  • What you get: recurring automated messages about CDL-A driving jobs, your application, and follow-ups.
  • How often: message frequency varies, up to 8 messages a month.
  • Cost: message and data rates may apply. We don't charge for texts.
  • Stop: reply STOP at any time to cancel. You'll get one message confirming you're unsubscribed, and no more texts after that.
  • Help: reply HELP, email drivewithtk6@gmail.com or call 443.741.2202.
  • Carriers are not liable for delayed or undelivered messages.

See our Privacy Policy for how we handle your information. Mobile information and SMS consent are never shared with third parties for marketing.
